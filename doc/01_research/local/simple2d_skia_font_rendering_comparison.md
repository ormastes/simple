## 2. Exact Root Causes of Showcase Visual Defects

### A. Mid-word character splits and overlaps (`"Mod ule"`, `"flo ated"`)

1. **Integer `by[i]` jitter vs phase-0 rasterization:**
   - Layout (`_text_pen_milli` / `measure_text_advances`) accumulates continuous milli-px pens `P[i]` and outputs integer steps `by[i] = round(P[i+1]) - round(P[i])`. As fractional advances accumulate across characters (`+0.4`, `+0.4`, ...), `round(P)` jumps inconsistently (`by[i]` swings by `+/- 1 px` relative to the glyph's true width).
   - `fb_text_ttf_aa_clip_range_adv` (`paint_primitives.spl:2692-2748`) places every glyph at integer `pen_x` using **phase 0** (`subpixel_x = 0.0`, `x0_px = floor(min_x)`). When `round(P[i])` rounds DOWN while `round(P[i+1])` rounds UP, the visual gap widens by ~1 px mid-word (`"Mod ule"`, `"flo ated"`). When the rounding flips opposite (`-0.5px` vs `+0.5px`) or pairs with negative `kern`/`lsb`, ink edges touch or overlap.
   - By contrast, Skia (`sfnt_rasterize_glyph_baseline_aligned`) preserves `subpixel_x = P[i] - floor(P[i])` inside `x0_px = floor(subpixel_x + min_x)` and resamples edges at `subpixel_x`, and `FontRenderer._lcd_apply_pen_phases` reconstructs `pens[i]` to assign quarter-pixel `phase_q in 0..3` + whole-pixel shift so consecutive glyphs maintain subpixel spacing.
2. **Fractional `font_size_milli` dropped on non-regular/bold/italic/`>48px` TTF rasterization:**
   - `_ttf_rasterize_glyph` (`paint_gradients_text.spl:740-760`) checks `if font_size <= 48 and not synth_bold and not synth_italic:`. Whenever `synth_bold` or `synth_italic` or `font_size > 48` applies, outline scaling falls back to `scale = (font_size as f64) / upem`, ignoring `font_size_milli` while layout measured `font_milli`.
3. **Non-`by` fallback drops kerning, fractional size, and cumulative pen:**
   - Whenever `use_by` is false (`paint_raster.spl:1875` sets `line_by = []` on `text_overflow_ellipsis`, or fallback paths), `_ttf_hmtx_advance_px` rounds each glyph independently at truncated `font_size` with zero kerning.
4. **`render_text` & `render_text_payload` (`font_renderer.spl:3212-3260`) diverge from `measure_text_advances`:**
   - `render_text` steps by integer `glyph.advance`, uses integer `horizontal_kern` (which returns `0` on `selected_outline_blob`), and does not apply `glyph.bearing_x`/`bearing_y` for vector glyphs.

### B. Right-edge text and badge collisions (`"HTML/CSS Render Parity"` overlapping `"Forms & Paint"`)

1. **Extraneous `+1 px` `text_ink_x_offset` on `#text` TTF runs (`paint_raster.spl:1857`):**
   - `paint_raster.spl:1857` sets `val text_x = x + text_ink_x_offset(st.font_size)` (`+ 1` px) unconditionally, even though `use_ttf` removed `text_ink_y_offset` at line `1858` and Draw IR (`paint_draw_ir.spl`) / TTF `<input>` (`paint_raster.spl:1802`) start at `x` (`+0`). Every TTF text node shifts `1 px` right toward its right border or neighboring inline/badge box.
2. **Synthetic bold smear (`bold_extra`) without advance/clip coordination (`paint_gradients_text.spl:894-1014`):**
   - When `!face.native_bold`, `_ttf_rasterize_glyph` expands bitmap right edge `x1_px = ceil(max_x) + bold_extra` (`+1` px `< 36px`, `+2` px `>= 36px`) and smears coverage rightward. `_ttf_hmtx_advance_px` adds `0` for synthetic bold, causing bold glyph ink to bleed `1..2 px` into subsequent glyphs and overrun narrow right-aligned / badge boxes when unmeasured (`use_by == false`).
3. **Engine2D 5x7 fallback collision (`glyph.spl:48-51`) & UTF-8 index bug:**
   - `glyph_advance(scale) = 5 * scale` leaves `0 px` spacing between adjacent 5x7 cells, unlike Skia's `6 * scale` (`ascii_glyph_advance_x` / `skia_bitmap_5x7_metrics`). Note: G2.5 (`cross_app_glyph_consistency_spec.spl`) and Vulkan dense bitmap atlas (`backend_vulkan_font.spl:910`) explicitly rely on `glyph_advance(scale) == 5 * scale` for contiguous 5xN atlas cells, so spaced parity must be exposed alongside (`glyph_spaced_advance`) without breaking `glyph_advance`.
4. **Shaping truncation and UPM scaling mismatches:**
   - `FontRenderer._resolve_selected_shaped_glyph_run` (`4504-4515`) truncates `total_advance.to_i32()` instead of rounding, under-estimating cluster/run width vs Skia `f64` sum.
   - `skia/feature/glyph/rasterize.spl:217-218` derives `scale = size_px / max(em_w, em_h)` instead of `units_per_em`, mis-scaling SFNT outlines whose glyph bounds are smaller than `units_per_em` (while `stub_box_outline` relies on bounds extent when `units_per_em == 0`).
   - `sfnt_measure_glyph_into` (`sfnt_glyf.spl:1024`) stores `hmtx.lsb` in `meta[3]` even though `_outline_edges` (`1087`) translates contours by `glyf.xMin` (`bounds.left`), causing `lsb - xMin` horizontal misplacement when `hmtx.lsb != glyf.xMin`.
