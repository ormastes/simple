#### treats deadline zero as never and saturates instead of wrapping

- A zero deadline is never reached
- A deadline is reached at and after its instant
- A huge timeout saturates at the maximum instant
   - Expected: sosix_time_deadline_after(10u64, 20u64) equals `30u64`
   - Expected: sosix_time_deadline_after(18446744073709551610u64, 100u64) equals `18446744073709551615u64`
